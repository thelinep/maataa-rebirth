The eight dollar tiers remain unassigned. Dollar denomination and the Saptdhatu conversion are not confirmed. No tier is a configured checkout amount.

Investment commitments and escrow deposits are separate financial records, not SKUs. Escrow workflow access covers planning tools only, without custody or fund release.
